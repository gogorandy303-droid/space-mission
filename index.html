Actions happening now

AFFIRMATIVE POWERSubject + am / is / are + verb-ingShe is playing tennis now.
They are watching TV at the moment.
I am reading a story now.

I → am · he / she / it → is · you / we / they → are

NEGATIVE POWER · EXTRA PRACTICESubject + am / is / are + not + verb-ingShe is not playing tennis now.
They are not watching TV at the moment.
I am not reading a story now.

Put not after am, is or are. You can shorten is not → isn’t and are not → aren’t. Keep the verb’s -ing ending.

Two special spellings from your worksheet

write → writingDrop the final e, then add -ing.
run → runningDouble the final n, then add -ing.

fly → flying: keep the y and add -ing.
